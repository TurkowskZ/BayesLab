Why achieving p < .05 often means your result is total garbage
The Transposition Delusion

Your p value only tells you: "If literally nothing was happening, would these numbers be bizarre?"
It does not tell you: "Did I actually discover something real?"
To flip that question around, you need priors. If your theory was wacky to begin with, a significant p value just means you likely struck an expensive false alarm.
